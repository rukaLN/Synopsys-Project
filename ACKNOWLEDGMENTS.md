# Acknowledgments

Walking Buddy was built by the project team, with assistance from
**Claude (Anthropic)**.

Claude specifically helped us:

- **Create the break-risk equation** shown on the app's home page — the
  weighted model (`BreakRiskModel`) that fuses walking speed, cadence, step
  length, steadiness, asymmetry, double-support, heart rate, and duration into
  a single 0–100% "break risk" score.
- **Design the Live Activity** feature (the Lock Screen + Dynamic Island card
  shown during an active walk).
- **Design the widgets** — the iPhone Home Screen / Lock Screen widgets and the
  Apple Watch complications.
- **Design the Smart Stack** integration, including the watchOS relevance that
  surfaces the walk widget while a workout is active.

All work was reviewed and integrated by the project team.
