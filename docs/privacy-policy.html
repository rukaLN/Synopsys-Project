Walker Buddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Walker Buddy helps you walk safely by watching your walking speed, steadiness and heart rate and reminding you when it's time to rest. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to that information.

The short version

Walker Buddy does not collect, sell or share your data. Everything the app uses stays on your iPhone, your Apple Watch and in Apple Health. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

Information the app accesses

  • Apple Health data (with your permission): walking speed, walking steadiness, walking asymmetry, double support time, step length, step count, walking distance, flights climbed, active energy, heart rate and date of birth (used to estimate a safe heart-rate range). The app also saves your walks to Apple Health as workouts.
  • Motion data (with your permission): used on your device to measure steadiness and detect fatigue during a walk.
  • Location (with your permission): used only so the app can keep monitoring your walk when the screen is off. Walker Buddy does not record, store or use your actual location.
  • Notifications (with your permission): used to alert you when you should rest.

How information is stored

Your walk history and settings are stored locally on your devices. Your iPhone and Apple Watch share walk information directly with each other using Apple's built-in connection between paired devices, and the app's widgets read a small summary from the same device. None of this information is sent to us or to anyone else.

Apple Health

Information from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties. You can change or remove Walker Buddy's Health access at any time in the Health app or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Your choices

You can turn off Health, Motion, Location or Notification access for Walker Buddy at any time in your device's Settings. Deleting the app removes the walk history it stored on your device. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

Children

Walker Buddy does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

Walker Buddy is a wellness tool and is not a medical device. Always follow the advice of your doctor or care team.
